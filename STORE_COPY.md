# Hoot, the fridge reminder owl

**Hook:** The fridge owl that blinks at you until you've taken your meds and had your water.

**Description (60 words):**
Hoot is a fridge magnet that remembers for you. Press its left wing when you take your meds, its right
wing when you drink water. When one comes due again, daily or every two hours, that eye blinks until
you press. A quiet owl means you're done. Gold ENIG eyes, yellow wings; two magnets and a year-long
coin cell included.

**Suggested price:** $16

1. A fridge door in morning light: Hoot among children's drawings and a calendar, its left eye glowing
   orange, a pill organiser on the counter below.
2. Close-up: a finger pressing the "water" wing, a glass of water in soft focus behind, the right eye's
   thank-you glow.
3. The gift shot: Hoot in a small kraft box with its two magnets and a handwritten tag, "for Mum: no more
   'did I take them?'".
